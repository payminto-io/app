## Rulings

- **NEW-C1.** `solana_sweep_deposits` is unique on `(sweep_id, deposit_id)` (model tag and migration `2026100708`, still unshipped). `sweepBatch` writes the sweep (status `processing`), its sweep transactions (pending, no hash) and the deposit links in one transaction before broadcasting; `recordAttempt` then writes the attempt row and flips the sweep to pending (three tries). A broadcast error fails the sweep and releases exactly its links. A DB failure after the broadcast writes a `solana_unrecorded_broadcast: sweep N` anomaly carrying the signature; `trackSweep` recovers a processing sweep with no attempt from that row (`recoverUnrecorded`) and books it normally. Deposits become swept only in `book`, through the links.
- **NEW-C2.** `last_balance_raw` is persisted only when the token-account poll explained the movement (at least one signature listed, or a held signature); otherwise `balance_hold_attempts` counts up and the account is re-polled every tick, and at `MaxHeldAttempts` a `solana_unexplained_balance` anomaly is written and the balance accepted. The second `pollAddress` after a spent hold budget now carries its own `heldError` into `held_signature`. Tests: both probes plus `TestSolanaDeposit_C2_UnexplainedBalanceMovementIsHeldThenFlagged`.
- **NEW-I1.** `rebuildOrFail` applies the balance guard before the `MaxAttempts` failure: any account at or below its claim while no attempt is finalized means wait. `explainDrain` accepts only signatures of sweeps still tracked; a signature of a sweep booked failed revives it (`solana_failed_sweep_landed` anomaly, attempt back to sent, sweep to pending) so the tracker books it once; signatures of completed sweeps do not explain a drain. Test: `TestSolanaSweep_I1_LastAttemptLandingBehindLaggingNodesIsBooked` (no churn after booking).
- **NEW-M1.** Expiry reads the balance once (`ListExpiring` + `getMultipleAccounts`), flags `solana_late_balance` when it exceeds recorded deposits, skips accounts with a held or unresolved signature, and schedules the expired account on `ExpiredScan` (daily); `scanExpired` flags any rise. Test: `TestSolanaDeposit_M1_ExpiryKeepsHeldAndFlagsLateBalance`.
- **NEW-M2.** Amounts are the claimed deposits' sum read against the finalized balance (`amountFor`): equal means transfer and close; more means transfer the claim and leave the account open for the next sweep (per-item `SweepItem.Close`); less is a drain. Rebuild applies the same rule. Test: `TestSolanaSweep_M2_MoneyBeyondTheClaimIsLeftForTheNextSweep` (decoded transaction: one transfer of 25, no close).
- **NEW-M3.** `NodeCaller` (`NodeIDs`, `CallOn`) on `PoolCaller`; `Client.GetTransactionFromDistinctNodes` reports how many distinct endpoints answered. `evidencedDrop` and sweep expiry require two; with one the deposit stays pending / the sweep waits, with a `solana_evidence_unavailable` anomaly. `ScriptedCaller.Nodes` lets tests declare endpoints (fixtures default to two). Tests: `TestClient_GetTransactionFromDistinctNodesCountsEndpoints`, `TestSolanaDeposit_M3_SingleEndpointNeverFailsADeposit`.
- **Lows.** L1: `late_payment` anomaly when a deposit finalizes for a closed payment (`TestSolanaDeposit_L1_LatePaymentIsAnAnomaly`). L2: the unresolved list is read from the account row, not `fmt.Sprint`. L3: `PoolCaller.Call` never sleeps; all nodes backing off returns `ErrRateLimited` (`TestPoolCaller_DoesNotSleepWhenAllNodesBackOff`). L4: outside live a mismatched mint has `deposit_enabled` flipped off, so assignment stops handing out its addresses. L5: failed deposits are re-checked only on the late cadence (`updated_at` older than `LateCadence`).
