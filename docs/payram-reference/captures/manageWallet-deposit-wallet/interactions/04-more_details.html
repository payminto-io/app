Wallet management/Deposit wallet

Deposit wallet

Your self-custody deposit addresses — derived from your master wallet and secured by on-chain sweeper contracts. Only you control the funds.

Deposit wallets let your customers pay to unique blockchain addresses that you fully own. Unlike hosted payment processors, PayRam never holds your funds — every deposit address is derived from your master wallet and locked by a FundSweeper smart contract that can only send funds to your cold wallet.

How it works
  1. 1
    Connect a self-custody wallet (e.g. MetaMask) — this becomes your Master Wallet and is used for all on-chain setup operations
  2. 2
    Fund your Master Wallet with gas (native token) on each blockchain you want to accept payments on
  3. 3
    Deploy the FundSweeper contract — a smart contract that ensures deposited funds can only be swept to your cold wallet address
  4. 4
    Set your Cold Wallet — a secure wallet where all consolidated funds are sent. Keep this wallet and its keys extremely safe
  5. 5
    Enable deposit options on additional blockchains using the same Master Wallet
Never lose access to your Master Wallet — it is the owner of all deployed FundSweeper contracts and cannot be changed.
Your Cold Wallet should be a hardware wallet or a highly secured address. All swept funds are consolidated here.
PayRam handles the orchestration (generating addresses, detecting deposits, batching sweeps) while the blockchain handles the security (smart contract enforces fund routing).
